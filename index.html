About Olenguruone Tea

Heritage • Quality • Kenya’s Highlands

Nestled in the lush Mau highlands of Kenya's Rift Valley, Olenguruone Tea Factory Co. Ltd is a premier KTDA-managed factory established to serve smallholder tea farmers across the region. Situated at high altitude with nutrient-rich volcanic soils, Olenguruone produces exceptional CTC black teas celebrated for their briskness, rich liquor, and vibrant aroma.

Production & Location

Strategically located in Olenguruone, Kuresoi South, Nakuru County, in Kenya's fertile high-altitude tea belt.

Our Products

We specialise in manufacturing and marketing premium C.T.C. black teas, across both Primary (BP1, PF1, PD, D1) and Secondary (F1, Dust, BMF) grades.

Governance & Management

Governed by a Board of Directors elected by shareholders representing six electoral areas, including a nominated gender director, and led day-to-day by a Factory Unit Manager supported by Production, Field Services, ICT and Accounting teams.

Serving Smallholders

As a KTDA-managed factory, Olenguruone exists to serve the smallholder farmers of the region — from green leaf collection through to export-ready tea.

Tea Processing Operations

Controlled CTC manufacturing process
1 · Wither 2 · CTC 3 · Ferment 4 · Dry 5 · Sort 6 · Pack
01

Withering

Fresh green leaf moisture is reduced under controlled airflow and temperature to achieve optimal pliability prior to CTC processing.

⚙Key controls: Airflow • Temperature • Time
02

CTC Processing (Cut–Tear–Curl)

Leaf is mechanically cut, torn and curled to uniform particle sizes, ensuring consistency required for export blending and brewing performance.

⚙Key controls: Roller pressure • Feed rate • Particle size
03

Fermentation (Oxidation)

Oxidation is carefully timed and monitored to develop colour, aroma and strength characteristics specific to each tea grade.

⚙Key controls: Time • Temperature • Humidity
04

Drying (Firing)

High-temperature drying halts oxidation and reduces moisture to safe storage levels, stabilising flavour and shelf life.

⚙Key controls: Inlet temperature • Outlet temperature • Residence time
05

Sorting & Grading

Tea is mechanically and manually graded into BP1, PF1, PD, D1 and secondary grades based on particle size and density.

⚙Key controls: Sieve size • Density separation • Visual inspection
06

Packing & Dispatch

Finished tea is packed, labelled and documented to ensure full traceability and compliance with buyer and export specifications.

⚙Key controls: Moisture verification • Batch coding • Documentation checks
✓

All processing stages operate under KTDA quality management systems with continuous monitoring and documented controls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on sourcing, shipping, and tea grades for international buyers

Olenguruone Tea Factory produces premium high-altitude CTC (Crush, Tear, Curl) black teas in several distinct grades tailored for global blenders and tea bag manufacturers:

  • BP1 (Broken Pekoe 1): Larger liquorish tea particles giving bright cup color, strong body, and smooth briskness. Ideal for loose-leaf tea packets and premium blends.
  • PF1 (Pekoe Fannings 1): Uniform fine particles delivering quick infusion, intense strength, and rich reddish-gold color. High demand for commercial tea bags.
  • PD (Pekoe Dust): Fine granular tea providing instantaneous brew strength, heavy body, and dark liquor depth.
  • D1 (Dust 1): Ultra-fine high-yield grade offering immediate full-bodied color and robust flavor.

We supply international buyers via direct export contracts and shipping through the Port of Mombasa:

  • Full Container Load (FCL): Minimum bulk export order is one 20ft container (~12,000kg to 13,000kg) or 40ft container (~26,000kg) in paper sacks with aluminum foil barrier lining.
  • Incoterms Supported: FOB Mombasa or CIF to your destination port worldwide.
  • Transit Logistics: Goods are transported securely via the main highway corridor from our Kericho factory to the Mombasa port container terminal with full tracking and phytosanitary clearance.

Overseas importers, tea packers, and distributors can source directly through our export desk or via the East African Tea Trade Association (EATTA) Mombasa auction. To initiate direct contract sourcing, fill out our Contract Desk Inquiry Form above or submit a trade application. Our export director will provide custom price quotations and contract schedules within 48 hours.

Olenguruone Tea Factory strictly adheres to international environmental, ethical, and food safety standards. We hold active certifications for:

  • ISO 22000:2018: Food Safety Management System for global food-grade safety.
  • Rainforest Alliance: Certified sustainable farming, biodiversity preservation, and farmer welfare.
  • Fairtrade: Ethical trading conditions protecting 10,000+ smallholder tea farmers in the region.
  • AFA / KEBS: Full regulatory export clearance and quality compliance guaranteed.

For international buyers, we accept standard secure trade instruments including Confirmed Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C at sight) and Telegraphic Transfer (T/T). All direct contracts follow standard EATTA and international trade guidelines for buyer security and quality assurance.

Environmental Stewardship

The Cycle of Light

In the early morning mist of the Kericho highlands, the first light of dawn touches the tea leaves, beginning the natural process of growth. For generations, we have watched the sun nourish our fields. But today, that relationship has evolved.

As the sun rises higher, our solar arrays begin their work. The same rays that gave the tea its flavor now provide the clean energy to wither, roll, and dry the leaves. This is the "Sun-to-Sip" cycle—a perfect loop where nature provides the power to refine itself.

By trading fossil fuels for sunlight, we are preserving the mountain air and protecting the rainfall patterns our farmers rely on. We don’t just harvest the crop; we harvest the sun.

System Status: 100% Clean Energy Processing
