1. Factory & Origin

Location: Kipchimchim, Ainamoi Division, Kericho County, Kenya

Processing Method: CTC (Cut, Tear & Curl)

Altitude: High-altitude Kericho Highlands

Annual Production: ~2.5 million kg made tea

2. Tea Grades

BP1 – Broken Pekoe 1

Largest grain; aromatic, light-bodied liquor; preferred for high-end blends.

PF1 – Pekoe Fannings 1

Medium-sized particles; bright, brisk cup; most common for blends & teabags.

PD – Pekoe Dust

Fine powdery grade; fast extraction; strong-bodied liquor.

D1 – Dust 1

Very fine strong grade; excellent for strong tea blends.
